# Surfers Point, Barbados, Barbados: Zed's Surfing Adventures

![Zed's Surfing Adventures](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/9ee854_73adc5897faf4edebb62f3b2ee7edea6~mv2.png/v1/fill/w_2500,h_1654,al_c/9ee854_73adc5897faf4edebb62f3b2ee7edea6~mv2.png)

Duration: Lessons are 2 hours; daily lesson plan would be self-assembled.

Price: USD 750 pp for 10 group lessons; lodging/food separate

Website: https://www.zedssurfing.com/surf-lessons-in-barbados

Housing: No all-in surf camp package found on official lesson page. Some related listings mention apartments, but book separately/directly.

Rating: 4.7 Google (69)

Lessons: Private USD 150 pp; group USD 75 pp; surfboards included.

Period fit: Strong warm-weather fallback; lessons bookable daily

Things to do around: English-speaking island, warm water, south coast beaches, Bridgetown, food, snorkeling, easy from Miami.

Notes: Great warm-water lesson fallback, especially for Miami-based traveler, but not a packaged two-week course.
