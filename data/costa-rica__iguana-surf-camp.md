# Tamarindo, Costa Rica: Iguana Surf Camp

![Iguana Surf Camp](https://iguanasurf.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/IGUANA-SURF-SHOP_08-4K.00_00_44_00.Still017.jpg)

Duration: Weekly surf camp package is 6 nights / 7 days; other pages advertise 7-night deals.

Price: About USD 1,498-1,698 pp for two weekly packages depending package/date basis

Website: https://iguanasurf.net/product/the-weekly-surf-camp-package/

Housing: Beach hotel shared/private options with breakfast; roundtrip Liberia shuttle on Saturday packages.

Rating: 4.9 Google (1667)

Lessons: Daily certified surf lessons, unlimited surfboard rental, nearby beach surf trip, rashguard, photo package, video analysis.

Period fit: Available-style weekly packages, but Sep-Oct is rainy season and less ideal for beginners

Things to do around: Tamarindo restaurants/nightlife, wildlife tours, beaches, estuary, ATV, sailing.

Notes: Strong school and easy from Miami, but late Sep-Oct is rainy season and can push beginners into less predictable conditions.
