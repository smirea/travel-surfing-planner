# Canggu, Bali, Indonesia: Elsewhere Surf Camp Bali

![Elsewhere Surf Camp Bali](https://elsewheresurfcamps.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/DJI_0101-5-scaled-1.jpg)

Duration: Daily/nightly package or weekly Sunday package; 14+ nights unlocks airport transfer on Sri Lanka side and Bali has weekly/nightly options.

Price: USD 1,017 pp for two weekly packages (USD 508 each), or about USD 1,040 for 14 nightly packages

Website: https://elsewheresurfcamps.com/bali-surf/

Housing: Dorm or private rooms, meals, drinks, theory, transport, gear, and social dinners included.

Rating: 5.0 Google (1942)

Lessons: Daily 2-hour surf lesson or guide, 2-3 guests per instructor, theory/video analysis, equipment and transport.

Period fit: Good through late Sep; October is still warm but edge of wet-season transition

Things to do around: Canggu cafes, gyms, nightlife, temples, Ubud, beaches. Very activity-rich but traffic/crowds are real.

Notes: Probably the most polished camp product, but far from both Miami and Berlin and Canggu is crowded.
