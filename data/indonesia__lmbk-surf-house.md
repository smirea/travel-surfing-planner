# Kuta, Lombok, Indonesia: LMBK Surf House

![LMBK Surf House](https://lmbksurfhouse.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Screen-Shot-2021-05-07-at-10.20.36-PM.png)

Duration: Surf-and-stay camp; weekly schedule recommended because activities rotate through the week.

Price: Quote required; 2026 guide does not publish package price in accessible text

Website: https://lmbksurfhouse.com/

Housing: Rooms with Wi-Fi, AC, terrace and hot water; airport pickup, breakfast, gym membership, family dinners included in package description.

Rating: 4.9 Google (420)

Lessons: Daily guided surf instruction/coaching, photo/video analysis, surfboard rental and insurance, car/boat surf transport, max about 2 students per coach in the water.

Period fit: Good for Sep-Oct; warm water and multiple south Lombok breaks

Things to do around: Kuta Lombok cafes, beaches, waterfalls, Pink Beach, south-coast viewpoints. Quieter than Canggu.

Notes: Very strong coaching description, but no public price found. Worth direct quote if Lombok appeals more than Bali.
