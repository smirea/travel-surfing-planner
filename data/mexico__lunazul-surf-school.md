# Sayulita, Mexico: Lunazul Surf School

![Lunazul Surf School](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5c21564555b02cf0eff89ee4/89005854-ad94-4753-b670-4fcd15b616a6/InWaterLessons_Lunazul-6.jpg)

Duration: 3-, 5-, and 7-lesson packages; 5-day progression includes final La Lancha session.

Price: MXN 11,092 pp for two 5-day progressions at 2-person rate; lodging/food separate

Website: https://lunazulsurf.com/lessons

Housing: No current official accommodation package found; Sayulita.com describes a surf camp concept but current official page is lessons only.

Rating: 5.0 Google (784)

Lessons: 90-minute lessons plus 1 hour practice after each lesson. 5-day two-person rate MXN 5,546 pp.

Period fit: Warm-weather fallback; lessons bookable, lodging separate

Things to do around: Sayulita restaurants/nightlife, Punta de Mita, La Lancha, jungle/beach trips, easy beach town vibe.

Notes: Good DIY option, but Sayulita can be crowded and this is not a clean all-inclusive course.
