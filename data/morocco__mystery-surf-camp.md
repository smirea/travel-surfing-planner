# Imsouane, Morocco: Mystery Surf Camp

![Mystery Surf Camp](https://mysterysurfcamp.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Surf-camp-Imsouane-e1720528300390.webp)

Duration: 8 days / 7 nights full surf package; repeat or request longer stay for two weeks.

Price: USD 1,403 pp if doubling the USD 702 8-day/7-night full surf package

Website: https://mysterysurfcamp.com/

Housing: Accommodation, meals, airport/bus transfers, towels, Wi-Fi, and surf equipment included.

Rating: 5.0 Google (495)

Lessons: 6 daily surf lessons, video/photo analysis every second day, surf skate lessons, free rental time.

Period fit: Strong for Sep 20-Oct 31; open year-round

Things to do around: Imsouane bay is quieter and wave-focused; less city/activity density than Taghazout or Agadir.

Notes: Great surf learning environment and reviews, but more remote. Better if the priority is mellow progression rather than nightlife or many non-surf activities.
