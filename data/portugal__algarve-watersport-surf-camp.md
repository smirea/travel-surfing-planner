# Lagos, Portugal: Algarve Watersport Surf Camp

![Algarve Watersport Surf Camp](https://algarvewatersport.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/woman-surfing-small-wave-cliffs-background-1024x683.webp)

Duration: 7 nights / 5 surf days beginner package; can be repeated for two weeks.

Price: EUR 1,560-EUR 1,760 pp for two weeks depending on Oct half; shared dorm base

Website: https://algarvewatersport.com/en/surf-camp-lagos-portugal/

Housing: The Kitehouse accommodation, breakfast, dinner, yoga, equipment, and surf transport included.

Rating: 4.9 Google (669)

Lessons: Zero to Hero beginner week has 5 group lessons, theory, pool paddle training, surf skate, and video analysis.

Period fit: Strong for Sep 20-Oct 31; April-October season

Things to do around: Lagos old town, beaches, cliffs, wake park, restaurants, boat tours, day trips around the Algarve.

Notes: Most complete Portugal package, but pricier than Ericeira and Fuerteventura. Good if comfort and meals matter.
