# Ericeira, Portugal: Ericeira Surf Camp

![Ericeira Surf Camp](https://ericeirasurfcamp.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Ericeira-Surf-Camp-Prices-Header.png)

Duration: 7-night beginner surf pack with 5 lessons; can be repeated for two weeks.

Price: EUR 819 pp for two mid-season beginner packs (7 nights + 5 lessons each)

Website: https://ericeirasurfcamp.com/prices/

Housing: Hostel/camp rooms in Ericeira; mid-season room prices also published separately.

Rating: 4.6 Google (120)

Lessons: Beginner surf pack includes 5 lessons per 7-night stay; rentals and extra activities available.

Period fit: Strong for Sep 20-Oct 31; mid-season pricing Sep 15-Oct

Things to do around: Ericeira town, Lisbon day trips, Sintra, Cabo da Roca, excellent food scene.

Notes: Best Europe logistics from Berlin and very good non-surf options. Water is cooler than Morocco/Canaries, so wetsuits are assumed.
