# Corralejo, Fuerteventura, Spain: Nomad Surf Camp Fuerteventura

![Nomad Surf Camp Fuerteventura](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/3b247c_267e150800464476a2aca87b2a6514d4%7Emv2.jpg/v1/fit/w_2500,h_1330,al_c/3b247c_267e150800464476a2aca87b2a6514d4%7Emv2.jpg)

Duration: 7-day surf-and-stay packages; two weeks gives 10 surf lesson days unless extra lessons are added.

Price: From EUR 510 pp for two shared-room weeks; private two-person room about EUR 538 pp for two weeks

Website: https://www.nomadsurfcamp.com/packages

Housing: Shared surf houses and private-room options around Corralejo; meals not listed as included.

Rating: 4.9 Google (855)

Lessons: 5 days/week of 4-hour daily surf lessons, equipment, certified instructors, insurance, and spot transfers.

Period fit: Strong for Sep 20-Oct 31; year-round Canary surf

Things to do around: Corralejo, dunes, Lobos island, volcano hikes, beaches, easy Europe flights.

Notes: Exceptional price/location fit. Weakness is 5 lesson days per week rather than daily lessons, but each lesson block is long.
