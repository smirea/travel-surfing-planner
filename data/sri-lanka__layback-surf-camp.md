# Weligama, Sri Lanka: Layback Surf Camp

![Layback Surf Camp](http://static1.squarespace.com/static/5c3c45545cfd79a26f6517bf/t/5ec0bb063ca6b4722cd648e7/1589689096424/sri+lanka+surfing+camp.jpg?format=1500w)

Duration: 7-night Sunday-to-Sunday packages; repeat for two weeks.

Price: EUR 698 pp basic / EUR 798 pp standard for two 7-night weeks; premium EUR 1,398

Website: https://www.layback.lk/surf-retreat

Housing: Dorm/private package options with pool, Wi-Fi, food inclusions depending on tier.

Rating: 4.9 Google (425)

Lessons: 5 morning lessons/week, surf theory, video analysis, 3:1 beginner ratio, spot transport, gear during lessons.

Period fit: Possible in late Sep-Oct; south coast peak is later, but surf is reported year-round

Things to do around: Weligama, Mirissa, Galle Fort, whale watching season later in year, cafes and south-coast beaches.

Notes: Excellent beginner setup but October is a season-transition risk; confirm conditions and rain before committing.

Sources:
- https://www.layback.lk/surf-retreat
